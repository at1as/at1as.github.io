Talk · AWS, Seattle February 28, 2024

Building Secure Search Infrastructure for Fraud and Trust Operations

I shared lessons from leading the team behind Twilio’s OpenSearch platform, which trust and safety teams used to investigate fraud, spam, and account takeovers.

I covered SAML, reviewer permissions, and audit logging for sensitive data, along with the indexing, ingestion, and cluster sizing choices that kept high-cardinality searches fast during traffic spikes.
