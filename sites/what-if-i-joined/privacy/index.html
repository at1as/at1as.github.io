PRIVACY

Privacy and redactions

I’ve removed email text, recruiter names and contact details from this site’s public pages and data.

Included

Selected company names, outreach quarters, campaign counts, company status at outreach and at its latest research check, public sources, dated share prices and valuations, and calculations using the pay package you enter. Historical status uses the full outreach quarter; an event within that quarter leaves the starting status uncertain.

Excluded

Recruiter names, email addresses, subjects, messages, excerpts, email links, message and thread IDs, exact contact dates, original job and team descriptions, private notes, quoted pay and personal financial history. The source workbooks and email archive are not part of this site.

Withheld company names

I withhold names where a search may be sensitive, the company is unclear, or the record needs review. These campaigns appear only in the total count. Their tickers, sources and valuations are also excluded so they cannot reveal a withheld name.

The names shown still identify companies associated with my recruiting history.

Your inputs

Calculations run in your browser. The site does not send your inputs to a server or save them in browser storage or URLs. The calculator has no analytics or session recordings. Reloading clears your changes; Reset inputs restores the example package.

Hosting

Loading the site requests its pages and files from the host. The host may log visits, including IP addresses. This is separate from the calculator, which keeps the amounts you enter in browser memory.

Source links

Source links open external sites when clicked. Those sites have their own privacy policies. This site loads no external trackers, fonts or market-data widgets.
