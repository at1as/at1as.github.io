Capability, adoption, and timing

A related breakthrough is not automatically fulfillment. Review must check the actual capability, how widely it was required to be used, the specified technology, and the original deadline. A prototype cannot establish a claim about routine or widespread use.

04

Research notes & history

The January 2010 tally is retrospective evidence, not a reason to treat the entire 2000s robot emergence as late. This is distinct from the stronger 2019 ubiquity forecast K080.

Open questions
