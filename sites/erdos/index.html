Reading guide

Resolved covers proofs, disproofs, and other accepted answers. Each catalog defines its own categories.

Lean checks mathematical proofs. A catalog can list a formal statement of a problem before it lists a checked solution.

Dates show when a catalog recorded a change. Model releases provide context. Individual proof records give the available attribution.

Sources and dates

Dates show the first and latest snapshots we have for each catalog. Results in the first snapshot may have been proved earlier.

Combined

Counts a problem once if any catalog marks it resolved. Each catalog joins at its first snapshot. After its last snapshot, its recorded results carry forward.
