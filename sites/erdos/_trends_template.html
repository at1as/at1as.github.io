How the numbers are counted

“Resolved” combines proved, disproved, and otherwise solved. The seven remaining statuses, including ZFC independence, are grouped as unresolved; they are not stages on a path to a proof.

A statement in Lean expresses the question in a formal language. A solution in Lean records a formal proof or disproof. Neither identifies who or what produced it; human review of the problem’s status can lag formalization.

We match problem numbers across the same snapshots as the Sankey. Repeated status changes are counted each time. Database additions and removals are counted separately.

$reconciliation

Daily averages count changes to existing problems. Each window starts at the last snapshot on or before its target start date. The count is divided by the actual days elapsed, shown below the chart.

Monthly bars count changes in the month they first appear. The first and last months may be partial. All categories add up to the net change.

Gaps include days without updates and revisions skipped for invalid status data. Changes between snapshots may be missed or recorded later. Duplicate problem numbers cannot be matched reliably; their effect appears as “unmatched changes.”
