The question

How far from the line could a zero lie?

Nontrivial zeta zeros lie between β = 0 and β = 1. A fixed zero-free region excludes a band next to each edge, at every height. Symmetry gives the left boundary from the right one.

Methodology

How to read
the charts

01

What the numbers measure

Each chart tracks a specific bound or set of cases. These quantities cannot be combined into a “percentage solved” or used to predict when a proof will arrive.

02

Dates and step changes

Lines step when a recorded bound changes. These are selected milestones, not complete record histories. Dates follow the cited announcement or paper; publication dates are noted separately.

03

Publication status

In the timelines, green marks published results or established constructions; amber marks preprints and reports. The region diagram uses shading for permitted and excluded locations, with evidence status shown separately. Full RH is an unproved reference. Status reflects the review date, even when a point is dated to an earlier preprint. The sources document the claims; this site does not independently verify the proofs.
